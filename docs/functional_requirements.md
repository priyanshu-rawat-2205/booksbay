# User Management
- Unified Profile: The system shall provide a single account type that enables a user to act as both a buyer and a seller without switching roles.
- Account Creation: The system shall allow users to register via Email/Password or OAuth 2.0 (Google/Apple) to ensure secure authentication.
- Reputation Score: The system shall maintain a public "Trust Score" for each user, updated based on post-interaction feedback from other users.
- Activity Dashboard: The system shall provide a unified view for users to track their active listings and their sent/received contact requests.

# Listings & Content Management
- Mandatory Metadata: The system shall require the following data for any listing: Title, Author, ISBN-13, Price, and Condition.
- Verification Images: The system shall mandate the upload of a photo of the Copyright Page and ISBN barcode to verify the physical originality of the book.
- Listing State: The system shall allow users to toggle the status of a book between Available, Pending, and Sold.
- Geo-Location: The system shall allow users to tag their general location (City/District) to facilitate local peer-to-peer pickups.

# Peer Connection & Discovery
- Search & Filter: The system shall enable users to search by ISBN, Title, or Author and filter results by price range and proximity.
- Request Contact: The system shall provide a "Request Contact" button that notifies the owner of the book that a user is interested.
- Direct Disclosure: The system shall reveal the owner's preferred contact detail (e.g., Phone or Email) only after the owner acknowledges the buyer's request.
- Transaction Disclaimer: The system shall display a mandatory safety notice before contact details are revealed, stating that all payments happen offline at the users' own risk.

# Trust, Safety, and Governance
- Piracy Reporting: The system shall provide a "Report Pirated Copy" feature on every listing to flag unauthorized or digital-print books.
- Behavioral Reporting: The system shall allow users to report peers for "No-show," "Deceit," or "Unprofessional Conduct" after a contact exchange.
- Auto-Moderation: The system shall automatically hide a listing from the public feed if it receives more than a defined threshold of piracy reports (e.g., 3 reports).
- Admin Adjudication: The system shall provide an administrative dashboard for moderators to review reports, delete listings, and ban repeat offenders.
